Add to Your Phone

Put Bridgewater YOU one tap away

Add Bridgewater YOU to your phone’s home screen and it opens like an app, with no typing of a web address. Choose your phone below.

Add to Your iPhone

Put a shortcut icon on your home screen.

  1. Use Safari, the blue compass icon, rather than another browser or app.
  2. Tap the ••• (More) button near the address bar, then tap Share. On older iPhones, tap the Share button instead: a box with an arrow pointing up at the bottom center of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If you don’t see it, tap View More first.
  4. Tap Add.

Add to Your Android Phone

Puts a Bridgewater YOU icon on your home screen, just like an app.

Use the Chrome browser.

  1. Open Bridgewater YOU in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu button (three dots) in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (on some phones it reads Install app).
  4. Tap Add (or Install) to confirm.

The Bridgewater YOU icon will now appear on your home screen. Tap it any time to open the site.
